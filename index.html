Highland Single-Track Driving Protocol

Much of the deep Western Highlands and Northern NC500 stretches utilize single-track roads. Always check ahead for passing places, let faster vehicles behind pass by pulling into laybys on your left, and maintain awareness over blind crests.

Midge Awareness Advice

Highland midges are common in valleys like Glen Etive and Glencoe when wind drops completely still. Keep car doors shut and apply protective repellant before stepping out.
