Our Story & Experience

Hi there, I’m CJ, born and raised on the sun-soaked island of Malta, but by now, i feel entirely Scottish at heart. Between running a full-time corporate job and keeping up with a busy family life, Scotland has become our ultimate escape, our slice of paradise, the place where one day we plan to retire.

My love affair with the country began on my first visit back in 2009. Since then, I’ve returned over 11 times, leaving a small piece of my heart behind with every single trip.

Scotland is woven into the biggest milestones of my life; I proposed to my wife on the quiet shores of Loch Eil, and we were married at St Mary’s in Edinburgh. I’ve driven the iconic North Coast 500 five times, yet my bucket list only seems to grow longer with every visit, top of the list being a full NC500 tour on a motorcycle (a dream that’s slightly trickier with a new baby in the mix, but never say never!).

I’ve traveled across dozens of European countries and visited the US four times, but nothing compares to the pull of Scotland. Maybe it’s the warmth of the people, maybe it’s the dramatic scenery, or maybe it’s just pure magic. Realistically, it’s a bit of all three.

We started Scotland Bound because we noticed a glaring issue with modern travel guides: they were either overly generic, written by AI bots that have never set foot (obviously) in the Highlands, or packed with unrealistic schedules that leave travelers exhausted behind the wheel.

Every route, tip, and stop in this guide comes from thousands of miles spent navigating single-track Highland roads, coastal loops, and island ferry crossings. We built this blueprint to give you a realistic, battle-tested, and beautifully paced driving masterclass, so you can experience the real magic of Scotland without the stress.
